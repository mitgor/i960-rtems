level loading from 1642.751 to 318.183 seconds (5.163×); the warm same-level
restart improves from 10.291 to 3.068 seconds (3.354×). Full audits remain.

The progress notes below are chronological. See **Final physical acceptance**
for the accepted build and **Interactive handoff** for the active game.

## Final physical acceptance

The repeat passed on boot `2607d8e0-ed30-4d8d-a8e3-85d31a2efde9`, supervisor 2167,
with the startup ordering correction and the exact final hashes above.

| Measurement | CRC mailbox WAD | Bulk WAD, no CRC | Improvement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| First frame after engine preparation | 1642.751 s | 318.183 s | 5.163×; 80.63% less time |
| Engine upload plus first frame | 1722.637 s | 397.148 s | 4.338× |
| Warm E1M1 restart, menu confirmation to new frame | 10.291 s | 3.068 s | 3.354× |
| Successful control exchanges, whole test | 4,187,858 | 246,615 | 16.98× fewer |

These are one controlled mailbox/bulk comparison, including full evidence logging
and scheduled audits. The upload-plus-startup metric excludes initial board
bring-up and network proof. The restart uses the same level, with 32 WAD requests;
it is not a claim about cold loading of every map. Scene-changing test blocks
cannot establish a CRC-on/off FPS gain, so the verifier marks that comparison
invalid. Earlier dedicated FPS results remain the reference.

All 52 framebuffers are **byte-identical** to the mailbox run. Four paired frame
DMA/mailbox captures match exactly. Native comparison has the same known seven
pixel differences, reduced to two/one when menus cover them; palettes and padding
match. All **14 full paired system/RAM audits**, owned STOP and global heap drain
pass. The Mac received all 52 binary frames, and every payload hash matches its
physical capture. The browser screenshot is `bulk-repeat-browser.png`.

Full WAD replay passes all 5,100 requests: 15,163,588 logical bytes carried in
20,889,600 fixed DMA bytes. Service calls total 72.544 s. Queries still cost
181.255 s over 11,408 ioctls; they are the largest measured remaining transport
cost. The short-poll bulk run is 8.18% faster than preliminary bulk startup, but
these runs also differ in startup scheduling and teardown correction; this is
not an isolated measurement of the polling change.

Linux full system/frame/WAD replays and 35 physical frame/journal mutations pass.
Native WAD tests reject 22 corruptions and cover descriptor/timeout/ownership
faults; no physical DMA fault was deliberately injected. Local replay independently
