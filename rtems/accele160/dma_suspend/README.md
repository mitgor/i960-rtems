# Active DMA suspend/resume after STOP

This is the next bounded lifecycle gate after the [finite-chain STOP test](../dma_chain_stop/README.md). The card uses the Intel 80303 channel-0 CCR Channel Enable bit to **suspend** an active 13,000-descriptor DMA chain when it observes a host STOP token. The software holds the channel for four 10 ms RTEMS ticks, captures DAR/NDAR/BCR after two and four ticks, then sets CCR Channel Enable again and requires the entire chain to finish. The host keeps both the pinned coherent buffer and stock-owned 512 KiB card grant throughout. Neither STOP nor CCR=0 is treated as a DMA abort.

On boot `a248da87-1bb3-403e-97fc-837b307e1b0e`, a fresh same-boot read-only inventory showed channel 0 inactive with its previous completed descriptor, stock channel 1 still occupied, and channel 2 idle. Two runs of the **same image** used separately pinned host buffers. In both, STOP was observed with CSR active at tick 21, CCR read back zero, settled DAR/NDAR/BCR snapshots at ticks 23 and 25 matched, CCR read back one on resume at tick 25, and the last descriptor completed at tick 52. The held DAR was `c0723b60`, short of the final `c07888e0`; BCR was 424 and 1104 bytes in the two runs. Each run wrote 26,624,000 bytes, repeatedly targeting an exactly admitted 4 KiB host payload page. The final CCR/CSR, NDAR and BCR were zero, DAR matched the final descriptor, the exact host payload matched and both guards were unchanged. Owner words, loader page, staging, RTEMS worker/ISR stacks, timer/vector and firmware continuation were audited before either allocation was released. End-to-end elapsed times including callback and host audits were 602.847 and 602.582 ms. No flash write or old-card access was issued.

Four guarded full 1 MiB firmware reads in pre/post pairs matched SHA-256 `1c4995ada54f944265c11f346177708351b4ff5a1003ba232f7e39b6a74b89bd`. The accepted image is `ba5b966c638937e85185095ff8415089101f626c71af20677e4bcbba44a8359b`; kernel is `2bb4640aabbdf5e8236f0e9a12756095b00149f58f974473f317aea413a329ca`. An independent rebuild was byte-identical. [Exact sources, binaries and captures](../../../artifacts/current/accele160-dma-suspend-20261017/README.md) are archived. Replay and twelve tamper cases:

The [Intel 80303 Developer's Manual, §19.10.3 and §19.14.1](https://bitsavers.trailing-edge.com/components/intel/i960/273353-001_Intel_80303_IO_Processor_Developers_Manual_May2000.pdf) states that clearing Channel Enable suspends at the earliest opportunity and the PCI interface may drain its queue; it does not promise an immediate stop of all bus writes. Stable settled registers show this finite chain paused and resumed under software control. This **does not** prove safe cancellation of a permanently stalled or erroring DMA transaction, process-death recovery, or safe unpinning while a channel remains active. On any uncertain result the runner retains the pin, grant and command modules. Those cases still need a separate recovery gate before persistent DMA service or Doom delivery on the new card.
